FAQ

Questions owners ask

What does the strategy call cost?

Nothing. It's a 20-minute call to see where your hiring is stuck and whether we're a fit. If we're not, we'll tell you.

What kinds of sales roles do you hire for?

In-home sales consultants, closers, canvassers and setters, sales managers, and inside sales and CSR roles for home service companies.

How fast can I get candidates?

It depends on your market and role, and we'll give you a realistic timeline on the call. Our process is built to get qualified finalists in front of you in weeks, not months.

Do you work in my area?

We recruit across the US. Tell us your service area in the application and we'll confirm on the call.

How is this different from posting on Indeed?

Job boards bring you whoever applies. We go after reps who are already producing, screen them for in-home sales ability, and only send you the ones worth your time.
